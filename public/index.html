Laws of the game

  • Teams: 11 v 11 (10 outfield players + a goalkeeper), 4-4-2.
  • Match: two halves of 45 minutes plus stoppage time for fouls, cards and goals. Teams change ends at half time.
  • Goal: the ball crosses the line between the posts and under the bar. The conceding team kicks off.
  • Out of play: throw-in to the other team; goal kick or corner depending on who touched it last.
  • Offside: in the opponent half and ahead of the second-last defender when the pass is played. Not from a throw-in, goal kick or corner.
  • Fouls: free kick; inside the box a penalty. Yellow and red cards; two yellows is a red. Sent-off players are not replaced.
  • Set pieces: opponents stand 9.15 m back at free kicks, throw-ins and corners.
  • Knockout: if level after 90 minutes, 2 x 15 minutes of extra time, then a penalty shoot-out.
  • Not implemented: substitutions, VAR, advantage, handball, injuries, the back-pass rule.
